Solid facts & calculator

A cube has 6 faces, 12 edges, and 8 vertices.

Volume64 units³
Surface area96 units²
Cube volume = side³. Surface area = 6 × side².
Volume = 4 × 4 × 4 = 64. Surface area = 6 × 4² = 96.

Note: the solids are educational visual models. The cube is rotatable; the other solids use labelled illustrations.
