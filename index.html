Question 1

What is the area of a rectangle with length 8 cm and width 3 cm?

Remember!

Perimeter is the distance around a 2D shape — like a fence.

Area is the space inside a 2D shape — like carpet.

Volume is the space inside a 3D object — like what a box can hold.

Units matter: cm for length, cm² for area, and cm³ for volume.
