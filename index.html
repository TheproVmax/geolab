What is area?

Area measures how much flat space is inside a shape. The green shading represents that space.

8 × 4 = 32

The shape covers 32 square units.

What is perimeter?

Perimeter is the total distance around the outside edge — like the length of a fence around a yard.

8 + 4 + 8 + 4 = 24

The distance around is 24 units.

Question 1
What is the area of a rectangle with length 8 cm and width 3 cm?

Remember the difference

Perimeter is the distance around a 2D shape. Think of a fence.

Area is the amount of flat space inside a 2D shape. Think of carpet.

Volume is the space inside a 3D object. Think of how much a box can hold.

Always include units: cm for length, cm² for area, and cm³ for volume.
